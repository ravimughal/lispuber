Simple process

Ride in 4 steps.

No learning curve. Open the app, tell us where to go.

1

Set your destination

Type where you want to go or tap the live map. Smart search suggests places instantly. GPS detects your pickup automatically.

2

Choose your ride & see the fare

Pick Economy, Comfort, or Premium. Price is locked in before you confirm — calculated by actual route distance, no surprises.

3

Get matched with a driver

Instantly connected to the closest verified driver. Watch them approach in real time — name, vehicle, plate, and rating visible upfront.

4

Ride, pay, rate — done

Payment is automatic. Leave a quick rating and you're done. A receipt hits your inbox immediately with a full breakdown.
