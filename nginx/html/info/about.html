Our story

We reimagined
city transit.

Founded in 2019 with one belief: getting around your city should be effortless, safe, and affordable for everyone.

Built differently.

Most ride platforms optimise for growth at the expense of experience. We chose the opposite - investing in driver quality, transparent pricing, and genuine safety before we scaled.

That meant slower early growth. It also meant that when riders discovered Lisp, they stayed. We earn trust one ride at a time.

Today Lisp operates in 40+ cities across Europe, with 85,000 active drivers and over 12 million completed trips, all without a single case of post-ride fare manipulation.

Our principles

  • Fares locked before confirmation
  • 3-step driver background checks
  • 24/7 in-app safety monitoring
  • Rider data never sold
  • Driver earnings reviewed quarterly
  • Zero tolerance for surge manipulation
